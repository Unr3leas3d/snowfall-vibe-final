# Snowfall Brand Guidelines (Nexaris Style)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This document overrides previous guidelines. The design target is a pixel-perfect match of **[Nexaris.com](https://nexaris.com)**, adapted for the Snowfall domain.

## Core Aesthetic
**"Modern Corporate SaaS"**
-   **Cleanliness**: Extensive white space.
-   **Typography-First**: Heavy, bold headings driving the hierarchy.
-   **Tech-Forward**: Use of deeply saturated blue (`#2160FF`) for high-impact areas contrast against stark white.
-   **Glassmorphism**: Subtle usage in the sticky header.

## 1. Color Palette

### Primary
-   **Impact Blue**: `#2160FF` (Used for "Call out" sections, primary buttons, and footer background).
-   **CTA Button (Hover)**: `#1A4FD1` (Darker variant for hover effect).
-   **Vibrant Gradient**: Often paired with a subtle cyan-to-blue gradient for "tech" accents.

### Neutrals
-   **Background**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White). Most sections should be white.
-   **Text - Headings**: `#0A0A0A` (Near Black).
-   **Text - Body**: `#4B5563` (Cool Dark Gray).
-   **Text - Inverse**: `#FFFFFF` (White) on Blue backgrounds.

### UI Colors
-   **Header Background**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
-   **Borders**: Very subtle `#E5E7EB` (Gray-200) for cards.

## 2. Typography

**Font Family**: `Inter` or `Plus Jakarta Sans`.
-   **Headings**: Extra Bold (800) or Bold (700).
-   **Body**: Medium (500) or Regular (400).

### Hierarchy
-   **H1 (Hero)**: ~3.5rem to 4.5rem. Center aligned. Heavy impact.
-   **H2 (Section Header)**: ~2.5rem. Center aligned.
-   **H3 (Feature Title)**: ~1.25rem. Left aligned in grids.

## 3. Layout Patterns

### Header
-   **Sticky**: Fixed at top.
-   **Minimal**: Logo on left. "Sign In" / "Get Started" on right. No cluttered navigation links in the center.

### Hero Section
-   **Alignment**: Centered.
-   **Elements**: H1 -> Subtext -> Email Signup Form (Input + Button Group).
-   **Visuals**: Floating 3D abstract shapes (Snow/Ice cubes/Spheres) flanking the content to create depth without obstructing text.

### Feature Sections
-   **Alternating Grid**: 50/50 Split.
    -   Row 1: Text Left / Image Right.
    -   Row 2: Image Left / Text Right.
-   **Spacing**: Large vertical padding (`py-24` or `py-32`) between sections.

### "Our Solution" Highlight
-   **Full Width Blue**: A section with the `#2160FF` background.
-   **Content**: White text. High contrast list of benefits.

## 4. Iconography & Imagery
-   **Icons**: Minimalist line icons (Lucide/Heroicons) inside rounded squares.
-   **Imagery**: High-fidelity UI mockups (Abstract dashboards) or 3D renders. **NO Emojis**.

## 5. Copywriting Tone
-   **Headline Style**: "The Future of Snow Management is Here".
-   **Tone**: Confident, innovative, efficient.
-   **Keywords**: "Automated", "Seamless", "Reliable", "Real-time".
